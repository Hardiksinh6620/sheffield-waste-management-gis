# Sheffield waste management: a GIS case study

**Hardiksinh Ajaysinh Parmar · MSc Geographic Information System · Sheffield Hallam University**

A college research project exploring waste-management practices in Sheffield through GIS mapping, a questionnaire survey, and field observations. This repository presents the dissertation and documents the evidence needed to reproduce its analysis.

**Current status:** report archive and research documentation. Original GIS projects, raw survey responses, field observations, and executable analysis scripts have not yet been recovered for this repository.

## Research questions

1. How effective are the waste-management practices considered in the study?
2. What areas for improvement are identified by the study's participants and analysis?
3. What spatial patterns are reported for waste generation, collection, and disposal in Sheffield?

## Selected reported findings

The dissertation describes differences in waste patterns across the study area and recommends locally tailored responses. Questionnaire participants expressed support for GIS in waste-management planning. These are findings reported in the original dissertation; the underlying datasets have not been independently reanalysed for this repository.

The effectiveness table on PDF page 31 reports the following responses:

| Response | Count | Reported percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ineffective | 11 | 15.7% |
| Neutral | 9 | 12.9% |
| Effective | 24 | 34.3% |
| Strongly effective | 26 | 37.1% |
| **Total** | **70** | **100.0%** |

Percentages are rounded as in the report. These opinions are not a measured estimate of citywide operational performance or proof that GIS caused improvements.

## Reproducing the study

Start with the [reproduction checklist](docs/reproducibility.md). There is currently no executable pipeline in this repository, and the maps cannot be regenerated from the PDF alone. Future recreations will identify their data, methods, and actual development dates separately from the original coursework.

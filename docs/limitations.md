# Limitations and interpretation

## Evidence limits

Only the final dissertation was supplied for this repository. The underlying GIS inputs, individual questionnaire responses, and analysis files have not been independently inspected or reanalysed.

The reported questionnaire sample contains 70 respondents. It should not automatically be treated as representative of Sheffield residents, all waste-management practitioners, or the city's measured operational performance.

## Claims that require additional evidence

- Agreement that GIS improves routing is an opinion reported by participants; measured routing gains require operational comparisons and defined metrics.
- A mapped association does not by itself establish that population, land use, or infrastructure causes a waste pattern.
- Broad claims about applicability to other cities need evidence about differences in data, services, geography, and institutions.
- The absence of original data and parameters prevents a full assessment of spatial accuracy, completeness, and sensitivity to analysis choices.

## Date uncertainty

The cover date is September 2022, while the PDF export metadata is from September 2023. The submission date remains unconfirmed. Do not use the repository publication date as the study date or assign undocumented historical research dates.

## Scope of this edition

This edition improves access to the original report and describes what is needed for further work. It does not silently correct its findings. Any later correction, reproduction, or updated analysis should explain the change and retain its own development date.
